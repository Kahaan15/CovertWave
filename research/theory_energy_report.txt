==============================================================================
  WHY ENERGY-WEIGHTED PLACEMENT REDUCES DETECTABILITY
==============================================================================
  Fixed 20000 LSB flips confined to one energy decile at a time,
  10 equal-population deciles, 512-sample frames.
  Decile 0 = quietest, decile 9 = loudest.


   decile   mean energy    |d|<=1   mean |d|   d(rs_diff)/bit    d(spa)/bit
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
        0         142.7     31.6%       25.2        7.723e-07     3.887e-07
        1         527.7     15.4%       77.0        4.141e-07     1.929e-07
        2         944.4      9.2%      128.1        3.015e-07     1.329e-07
        3        1249.2      5.8%      197.1        2.350e-07     9.457e-08
        4        1588.9      4.1%      265.8        1.576e-07     4.849e-08
        5        1979.5      2.6%      296.5        7.177e-08     1.858e-08
        6        2429.1      1.6%      294.9        2.385e-08     4.372e-09
        7        2994.8      1.2%      371.0       -2.547e-08    -8.342e-09
        8        3833.7      1.0%      451.4       -2.765e-08    -7.936e-09
        9        5549.1      0.7%      696.7       -3.377e-08    -1.357e-08

  QUIETEST vs LOUDEST decile, per embedded bit:
    RS  rs_diff: 7.723e-07 -> 3.377e-08   (22.9x less perturbation in the loudest decile)
    SPA |beta|: 3.887e-07 -> 1.357e-08   (28.6x less perturbation in the loudest decile)
    fragile pairs (|d|<=1): 31.6% -> 0.7%
    mean |adjacent difference|: 25.2 -> 696.7

  Spearman(energy, |perturbation per bit|) for RS: rho = -0.879, p = 0.0008

  Spearman(energy, |perturbation per bit|) for SPA: rho = -0.891, p = 0.0005

  A negative rho confirms the prediction: the same number of embedded
  bits perturbs the detectors less when placed in higher-energy frames.
  This is the mechanism the RMS weighting exploits, and it accounts for
  the ablation result (+25.0 pp for energy weighting, -11.4 pp for
  randomisation): randomisation relocates bits without regard to
  fragility, whereas energy weighting moves them away from it.
