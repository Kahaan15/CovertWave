  2. DETECTOR CALIBRATION AND POWER
==============================================================================
  Threshold = 95th percentile of the clean-cover distribution,
  i.e. each detector is calibrated to a 5% false-positive rate.

  detector         threshold   FPR (clean)    TPR @50%     power
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  chi_square        1.000000         0.0%        0.0%    NONE (saturated)
  rs_analysis       0.016474         6.8%       56.1%              strong
  spa               0.008532         6.8%       43.9%              strong
  histogram         0.982426         6.8%        6.1%                NONE

  A detector whose TPR does not exceed its FPR is not detecting anything.
  Chi-square saturates on 16-bit audio: the LSB plane is already
  noise-like, so clean covers max out the pairs-of-values statistic and
  embedding has no headroom left to push it further.

  3. CLEAN-COVER BASELINE (the control the original study lacked)
==============================================================================
  Clean covers scoring SAFE (0 detectors fired): 36/44 = 81.8% [95% CI 68.0-90.5%]
  Mean discriminative detectors triggered      : 0.205

  Under the ORIGINAL battery this baseline was 63.6% 'survival' — the same
  as stego at 1-10% payload — meaning it could not separate cover from
  stego at all. A calibrated battery must sit near 100% here.
